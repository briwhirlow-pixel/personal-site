001 · Positioning
Voice / Tone

You are the one-person studio that hand-builds.

Every piece of content should reinforce one core idea: BuiltbyBrian is a real human, in Philly + South Jersey, who hand-builds custom websites for local businesses — fast, fairly priced, and you own the code forever. That's the moat against Wix, Squarespace, and faceless agencies. Lean into it relentlessly.

Brand voice

Editorial. Direct. Anti-fluff.

  • Italic serif for emphasis — mirrors the site's existing voice.
  • Short declarative sentences. Numbers and specifics over adjectives.
  • "You" focused. Always talking to the small business owner, never about them.
  • Confident but not flexing — proof + transparency over hype.
Don't say

The fluff list — avoid

  • "Take your business to the next level"
  • "Cutting-edge / world-class / game-changing"
  • Made-up stats or revision counts not on the site
  • Generic AI-flavor phrases ("In today's digital landscape…")
002 · Content pillars
Rotation / 5

Five buckets. Rotate every week.

Mix pillars across the week so the feed feels alive. Education builds trust at scale; reveals close the deal; founder + local posts make you human.

Education
Carousels and tip posts. "5 mistakes restaurants make on their menu page." "Why your site isn't ranking." This pillar does the heaviest reach lifting and earns saves + shares.

10%

003 · Cadence
Posting / week

The schedule that keeps you visible.

Feed cadence

3–4 posts per week, minimum

Monday: Education carousel (heavy save rate).
Wednesday: Build-in-public or Reel.
Friday: Founder, proof, or local post.
Sunday (bonus): Direct offer / open slot.

Stories

Daily, 2–4 frames

WIP screenshots, polls ("Which homepage feels stronger?"), behind-the-scenes from your day, repost client mentions, milestone screenshots (Lighthouse 100s, new launch).

Reels

1–2 per week (algorithm boost)

Short, fast cuts. Screen recordings + a voiceover. 15–30 seconds is the sweet spot. Use trending audio when it fits.

Best windows · ET

Local-biz scroll hours

  • Tue–Thu 11:30am–1:00pm (lunch scroll)
  • Tue–Thu 7:00–9:00pm (post-dinner)
  • Reels: Wed–Fri 6–9pm hit hardest
  • Avoid Saturday mornings (lowest engagement for B2B local)

007 · Off-feed growth
Multipliers

Posting is half. The other half is leaving the post.

Engagement loop · daily 15 min

Comment to be seen

  • Follow 30–50 local Philly + SJ small biz accounts (restaurants, gyms, shops, salons).
  • Leave 10 thoughtful comments / day on their posts — not "🔥" but actual sentences.
  • Your name appears in their follower's notifications. Profile clicks compound.
Cold DM · 5/week

Soft, specific, no pitch

  • Find a local biz with a weak site you'd actually love to rebuild.
  • Send a personalized note. ONE specific compliment + ONE specific observation.
  • No price, no link, no ask the first message. The hook is curiosity.
Cross-channel · free reach

Recycle every post

  • LinkedIn — repurpose every educational carousel. Different audience, same content.
  • Facebook (your Marketplace base) — post launches and Reels into local Philly biz groups.
  • Threads — break carousels into a 3-post thread for second-wave reach.
Tag everything

Borrowed audiences

  • Tag clients on every reveal post (APEX first).
  • Tag location every time (Philadelphia / specific SJ town).
  • Tag tools used (@vercel @supabase @resend) — they sometimes reshare.
